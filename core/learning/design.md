# Contracts and tradeoffs
Select contract design or tradeoff design.
For contracts/tests, let the learner derive observable behavior, assumptions,
invariants, boundary examples and falsifiers before implementation. Checks must
assess outcomes rather than merely repeat the implementation.
For tradeoffs, obtain needs and constraints, a proposed choice and at least one
alternative. Compare benefits, costs, failure modes and recovery against the
constraints; let the learner defend and revise the choice.
Record reasons, evidence and unresolved questions. Review the decision using
the domain's correctness criteria and preserve relevant negatives.
