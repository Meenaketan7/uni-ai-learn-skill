# One hint at a time
Read the existing attempt and hint level. If no adequate attempt exists, ask a
smaller starting or prerequisite question. Do not reveal the answer while asking.
Level 0: no hint. Level 1: orient attention. Level 2: name a principle or ask a
leading question. Level 3: reveal a partial step.
Advance at most one level after observed need and an explicit hint request.
At level 3, seek a revision or offer a different partial step without completing
the solution. A complete answer still requires an attempt plus explicit request,
or task shipping bypass. Preserve level across compact and session resume.
