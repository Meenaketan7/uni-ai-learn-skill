# Prepare compact
Collect identity, mode/bypass, objective, constraints, acceptance criteria,
workflow and next action. Retain decisions with reasons and sources; failed
approaches with observations and explanations; open questions and needed evidence.
While learning, also retain attempts, hypotheses, corrected misconceptions,
hint level, internal intent and difficulty evidence even if logging is off.
Keep critical constraints in the resume capsule. Unique conversation facts must
be inline or in owned durable evidence, never a pointer that requires a new query.
Record typed source pointers with identity/revision/retrieval method and audit
what is retained, pointed or dropped and why. Validate and save before invoking
a native compact procedure or preparing a capsule for a fresh session.
Saving a checkpoint is not native compaction and does not clear live context.
