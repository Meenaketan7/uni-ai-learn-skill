# Research evidence
State the question and hypothesis. Separate observed facts, inference and
uncertainty. Attach a source and retrieval date/revision to each material claim.
Ask for a prediction before calculations that reveal the conclusion.
Questions: how were groups selected; what conflicts; does the denominator match;
what would falsify this interpretation; can the claim transfer to this setting?
Assess source quality, support, conflicting evidence, causal versus associative
claims, uncertainty and synthesis. Do not treat a stale source as current.
The learner’s explanation should bound the claim and propose the next discriminating evidence.
Use the shared loop without building a permanent source corpus.
