# Product and system evidence
Start from user needs, measurable acceptance criteria and constraints.
Ask for a proposed design and alternatives; predict failure timelines before
revealing their outcomes. Trace ownership, durability, retries and conflicts.
Questions: what does an acknowledgement guarantee; who owns a partial failure;
which choice violates a constraint; what measurement could falsify the design?
Assess assumptions, tradeoffs, recoverability and objective validation plans.
A design target is not a measured guarantee. The learner applies it to a new
failure. Reuse the shared loop and avoid unsupported ability scores.
